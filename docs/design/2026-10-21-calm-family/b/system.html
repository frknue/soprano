Folio

Primary appearancePaper (light)Ink (dark)

Each app reads like a well-set page: paper and ink, a serif voice for titles and one sienna pencil mark wherever your keyboard is. Mood: iA Writer (ink on paper), Things 3 (calm macOS density), Stripe Press (warm paper, serif heads, hairlines).
